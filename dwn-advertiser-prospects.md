# DWN advertiser prospects from daytondailynews.com

**Goal:** Find businesses that (1) already spend money on advertising and (2) have a reason to reach DWN's audience, using the Dayton Daily News (DDN) website as the evidence source.

**Researched:** October 15, 2026

**Assumption:** DWN's audience is Dayton and Miami Valley residents, which overlaps with DDN's readership. If DWN serves a narrower niche (for example seniors, families, business owners or a specific neighborhood), use the "Audience fit" column to filter the list.

**Method note:** daytondailynews.com could not be loaded directly from this research environment. All evidence comes from search-indexed DDN pages. Before outreach, open each linked page to confirm the "Sponsored Content" label and the sponsor name.

---

## How to read the tiers

| Tier | Budget signal | Confidence |
|---|---|---|
| **A – Proven DDN spender** | Bought a piece labeled "Sponsored Content" on DDN (native advertising, the most expensive digital format DDN sells) | High |
| **B – Likely paid Best of Dayton package** | Best of Dayton 2026 winner with a standalone profile or "sneak peek" story (Cox First Media sells Best of Dayton sponsorship/winner packages) | Medium; confirm the label on the page |
| **C – Marketing-active winner** | Won Best of Dayton 2026 (ran a vote-getting campaign; a natural buyer of "winner" ads) | Lower; budget is inferred |
